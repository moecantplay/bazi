Your day, three ways

Daymaster now comes in three looks. Your reading stays the same; pick the one you'd like to open every morning.

Picking a look

Onboarding

The last step before your chart is revealed. Swipe through three previews of your own day and tap one. The button says what it will do. The reveal and everything after it open in the look you picked.

Settings

A Look section under Appearance with the same three previews as small tiles. Tapping one switches Today straight away, no reload.

Existing users

Shown once, over Today, the next time they open the app. Keep Explorer or pick another; either answer means it never shows again. People who chose in onboarding never see it.

In the build

These previews are screenshots. The real ones are live, scaled-down renders of the person's day, so they follow their terrain and theme.
