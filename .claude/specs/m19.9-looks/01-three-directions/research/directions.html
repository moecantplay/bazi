Direction A · evolution

Trail, distilled

Keep the route metaphor, but make the map the first screen instead of one card among many. Date, headline and terrain are one surface. The route draws itself on arrival and a solid line shows how far into the day you are. Only two marks sit on it: the easy hour and the rough hour.

  • The four-section rail becomes three swipe cards, each saying a different thing.
  • The "What the marks mean" links go, because the marks label themselves.
  • The elevation strip moves below the fold.

Direction B · boldest type

Almanac page

Each day is a printed page: a large day number, the day's animal cut out at poster scale on a grained element field, and the headline set big. There's almost no chrome. It's the most Co-Star-like option and makes the best share image.

  • The map, rail and elevation strip retire from Today; the details sit in one flat list below.
  • The animal art carries the identity, so icon craft matters more.
  • Risk: it's quieter, and the data is less visible at a glance.

Direction C · signature object

Day dial

A 24-hour ring with the twelve two-hour animals round the rim, the way the almanac counts the day. The easy and rough hours are arcs, a live hand shows now, and today's animal sits at the centre. It reads in one glance, like a watch face.

  • It replaces the map hero and turns the timed facts into the hero.
  • The week becomes seven tone rings, which fits the dial.
  • It could become a widget or a watch complication later (M23).

All three

The first screen has a headline, one idea (about 30 words) and one thing to do. Everything else is one tap or swipe away. The idea is said once, down from four times now.

Same tokens, fonts and icon art as today. The only cinnabar is the seal. The nav never covers the hero. One arrival moment under 1.2s, springy press states, and fully static with reduced motion.
