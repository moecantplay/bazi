# Research 2026-07-29 — design-reference apps for the M18 design reset

Four parallel research agents (one per candidate direction + a category sweep),
findings cross-organized here. Every app was verified live in 2026; unverified
candidates were dropped. Purpose: the owner browses these, screenshots what they
love, and that (plus a direction pick) unblocks the M18 mockups.

**How to use this list:** don't read it all. Start with the shortlist, open the
store pages on your phone, and screenshot anything that makes you feel "I want
Daymaster to feel like *this*." 5–10 screenshots is plenty.

## Direction A — editorial / stark (typography-led, near-monochrome)

- **Co-Star** — astrology. Black/off-white, blunt grotesque set large, copy-heavy
  journal scroll, zero cards. Study tone-through-restraint; don't copy the mono
  palette — ink/cinnabar/element-hue is already a stronger hand.
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-hyper-personalized/id1264782561
- **Bear** — notes; 2× ADA winner for typography craft: tuned line-height,
  measure, single accent. Typographic tuning as the product. https://bear.app/
- **Things 3** — tasks; 2× ADA. Hierarchy from weight + spacing, no lines/boxes;
  micro-interactions without chrome. https://culturedcode.com/things/
- **SSENSE** — luxury fashion. Basis Grotesque, black-on-white, editorial
  whitespace — how a grotesque + air makes utility read premium.
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ssense-shop-designer-fashion/id1418754101
- **Readwise Reader** — read-later. E-reader canvas discipline (~65-char
  measure, generous margins) — the model for our read-more deep dives.
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/readwise-reader/id1615498124
- **Motivation — Daily Quotes** — one huge quote per screen; the daily-card
  model, swipeable and share-first.
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/motivation-daily-quotes/id876080126
- **Reflect** (https://reflect.app/) and **Minimal** (https://minimal.app/) —
  monochrome canvases where well-set text is the only visual interest.
- **Stoic** — daily reflection; quiet type, one prompt at a time. Also listed
  under ritual structure below.
- **Print roots:** Kinfolk and Cereal magazines are the source vocabulary of
  this whole direction (measured grotesques, quiet-not-empty restraint).
  https://readcereal.com · Kinfolk redesign:
  https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/kinfolk-tenth-anniversary-redesign-schick-toikka-publication-graphic-design-230621

## Direction B — full Material 3 Expressive

State of the language, mid-2026: it's "Material 3.5" — an evolution, not a new
major version; shipped system-wide with Android 16 QPR1 (Sep 2025), first-party
app redesigns largely complete by Dec 2025, third-party adoption still thin.
**Critical caveat that shapes our mockup:** reviewers consistently pan the
redesigns that only swapped in tonal containers ("boxes everywhere") — which is
roughly what our M16 pass did — and praise the ones that lean into motion and
organic shape. A direction-B Daymaster must be the second kind.

- **Fitbit** — the single best-regarded M3E execution: morphing shapes, floating
  toolbar, fluid page transitions. Model for reading-card → deep-dive motion.
  https://9to5google.com/2025/11/09/fitbit-material-3-expressive/
- **Google Photos** — cycling shapes on pull-to-refresh, wavy progress
  indicators — organic progress instead of mechanical bars (streaks, loading).
- **Google Calendar** — date rows as rounded tonal containers, typed FAB menu —
  the most directly relevant screen anatomy for a date-centric app.
- **Gmail** — cleanest "container" language; pill search bar pattern.
- **Google Meet** — oversized single-primary-action screens.
- **Files by Google** — animated home carousel (a week-strip pattern), pill toolbar.
- **Pixel Studio 2.0** — pill-grouped action clusters in a tool-dense UI.
- **Material You Widgets** (3rd-party pack) — evidence dynamic color can carry a
  whole system: our element-derived accent (wood/fire/earth/metal/water) would
  play the role Material's wallpaper color plays here.
  https://www.androidauthority.com/material-3-expressive-widget-pack-3588194/
- Rollout roundup with screenshots of all of the above:
  https://9to5google.com/2025/11/17/google-material-3-expressive-redesign/

## Direction C — modern almanac (ink / paper / seal as the system)

Tier 1 — closest to the target look:
- **Koyomi Tayori (こよみだより)** — daily solar-term + micro-season almanac:
  seasonal haiku, watercolor art, moon phase, progress bar through the named
  period, and a **sumi (ink) theme**. The strongest single reference for this
  direction. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/koyomi-tayori-japan-calendar/id6759728786
- **72 Seasons (くらしのこよみ)** — one day = one named micro-season as a
  full-bleed artwork + poem; the "today is a named period" hero framing is a
  direct analog to our day-pillar hero. Coverage:
  https://www.slowdown.media/article/this-app-tracks-the-seasons-of-the-japanese-lunar-calendar
- **Xichuangzhu 西窗烛** — classical Chinese poetry in a ruthlessly clean
  layout; seal-script and calligraphy as accent artwork, never kitsch.
  https://mwm.ai/apps/xi-chuang-zhu-pin-wei-zhong-guo-shi-ci-zhi-mei/912139104

Tier 2 — the authentic 通勝/黃曆 pool (content grammar, not layout polish):
- **通勝 2026 擇日萬年曆 (Firm 13)** — the genuine professional almanac: how a
  real 通勝 deploys red (headers, 宜/忌 markers) and structures a day. This is
  the source grammar our "Favors/Watch" layer translates. App Store id339679729.
- **Almanac Chinese Lunar Calendar (Calendar2U)** — the cleanest bilingual
  stem/branch/element field layout; a hierarchy reference for English-first
  almanac data. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/almanac-chinese-lunar-calendar/id1495819707

Tier 3 — texture and restraint benchmarks:
- **Zen Brush 2** — the best digital sumi ink/paper texture in production; near
  zero chrome. The hero-surface texture reference.
  https://apps.apple.com/app/id1012274888
- **Tide 潮汐 (MORELESS)** — "maximum expression, minimum elements"; one image
  mass per screen at production quality.
  https://mwm.ai/apps/tide-sleep-focus-meditation/1077776989
- **Ye Almanack** — Western modern-almanac framing with a seasonally-adaptive
  palette (design detail unverified — screenshots were thin).
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6757980451
- **Koyomi – Simple Calendar** — Japanese minimal vertical-scroll rhythm for
  list/week views. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/koyomi-simple-calendar/id6572303117

## Category sweep — astrology, ritual, and award winners (any style)

Direct category:
- **The Pattern** — study the **Transit Ring** (radial time-based transits —
  analogous to luck pillars/annual transits) and Bonds charts; skip the teal
  dark-mode look. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-pattern/id1071085727
- **CHANI** — study the Today/Week/Year time-horizon tabbing (close to our
  horizons); the visual system itself is safe/undistinctive. https://www.chani.com/app
- **Sanctuary** — business-model comparison only (live readings); low priority.
- **BluePrint — Chinese BaZi** (https://blueprintbazi.com/) and **Ming**
  (https://askming.app/) — the two direct BaZi competitors. BluePrint reads as
  generic wisdom-app treatment (a floor to clear, not a ceiling); Ming is worth
  studying for how it hedges classical claims in UI copy ("most likely" — close
  to our own voice discipline). Both merit a manual screenshot check.

Ritual / tone siblings:
- **grug** (see shortlist) — 2026 ADA Delight & Fun. Hand-drawn custom typeface
  as the entire design budget; six elemental themes (a near-parallel to five
  elements); one wisdom line at sunrise; zero login. The single most relevant
  award winner. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/grug/id6751649802
- **How We Feel** — the Mood Meter 2-axis diagram + 100-illustration system;
  the model for diagramming elements × polarity.
  https://www.behance.net/gallery/145365127/How-We-Feel
- **Gentler Streak** — 2022 Apple Watch App of the Year; turns data into
  supportive daily prose with explicit no-guilt framing — our agency-line tone
  at scale. https://www.sketch.com/blog/gentler-streak/
- **Stoic** — morning/evening ritual cadence; conventional visuals, fragile
  retention (a cautionary note our streak chrome already answers).
- **Focus Friend** (Hank Green) — Google Play Best of 2025 Best App: one
  character, one mechanic, deep warmth — same lesson as grug: craft the one
  moment, don't add surface area. https://focusfriend.me/
- **Moonlitt** — 2026 ADA Interaction: high-fidelity moon view with astrological
  overlays and a date-slider — study for the chart/orbit screen (its Liquid
  Glass material itself is Apple-trend, not our system).
  https://apps.apple.com/us/app/moonlitt-moon-phase-tracker/id6444718902
- **Tide Guide** — 2026 ADA Visuals & Graphics: full-screen animated tide
  charts, "visually distinctive without making data harder to read" — the exact
  problem of the pillar grid and luck timeline. (Award corroborated by three
  sources; direct store link unverified.)

## Cross-cutting observations

1. **The genre's award winners (grug, Focus Friend) are single-moment apps that
   spent everything on craft** — bespoke type, one character, one daily beat.
   Daymaster's daily reading is that moment; the reset should concentrate
   spend there, not spread it.
2. **Direction B has a named failure mode** — container-swap Material reads as
   shallow (the critics' consensus, and our M16 experience). A direction-B
   mockup must lead with motion, organic shape, and element-derived color.
3. **Direction C has a production-quality existence proof** — Koyomi Tayori's
   sumi theme + Xichuangzhu's calligraphic restraint + Zen Brush's texture
   together cover exactly the "contemporary, not antique" worry.
4. **Whatever direction wins,** The Pattern's Transit Ring, How We Feel's Mood
   Meter, and Tide Guide's data-as-hero belong in the layout conversation for
   chart/cycles screens — they're direction-agnostic.
