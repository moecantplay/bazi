# Settings: everyday choices first

Status: draft · Milestone: M20 · Ticket: 19

## Problem

Settings (2026-09-29) opens with two calculation-school toggles ("Shift late-night births to the next day", "Use true solar time") above Appearance and Look. Those toggles matter to a handful of readers who know their school. Everyone else meets jargon first and has to scroll for theme and look.

## Goal

Settings leads with what most people change (look, appearance, data), and the calculation toggles sit under a clearly labelled advanced section.

## Requirements

- **R1.** Order: Look, Appearance, Your data, Install, About, then "How your chart is calculated" (the two toggles), then Delete.
  - Acceptance: mockups in all three looks × both themes approved before code; M19.9-09 builds on them.
